Context: the page “Long and Short Vowels”

Hear, pronounce, and spot spelling signals for long vowels (ie, silent h) and short vowels (double consonants).

Miete or Mitte? Length Changes Meaning

Walking to the Gymnasium in Lindenberg, Sam asks Lukas: 'Wohnen wir in der Miete or in der Mitte?' Lukas laughs: vowel length turns similar-looking words into different meanings — and German spelling almost always signals which one you need.

Sound-Spelling Board: Signals for long and short vowels:

  • Vowel length | Spelling signal | Examples
  • Long (tense) | ie (for long ee) | Miete, spielen, Biene
  • Long (tense) | silent h (Dehnungs-h) | Fahrrad, wohnen, Lehrer
  • Long (tense) | double vowel or single consonant | Kaffee, Boot, Tag, Leben
  • Short (lax) | double consonant, ck, or tz | Mitte, Bett, Bäcker, Platz

Remember: Never pronounce the silent h!

In Fahrrad, wohnen, or Uhr, the h is completely silent. Its only job is to lengthen the vowel before it.

Exercise stummes-h-erkennen

  • Task: Which word has a long vowel signaled by a silent h?
  • Answer format: choose one answer
  • Choices (✓ right, ✗ wrong, with the app's message):
    • ✓ wohnen – Correct! The silent h after o signals a long oh.
    • ✗ Wonne – The double nn makes the o short.
    • ✗ Sonne – Here too, nn signals a short vowel.
  • Solution: wohnen
  • Explanation: A silent h before l, m, n, r lengthens the vowel (wohnen, Fahrrad, nehmen); a double consonant (nn, tt, mm) shortens it.

Exercise minimalpaare-laenge

  • Task: Match each word from Sam's school day to its vowel-length signal.
  • Answer format: match each item on the left with one on the right
  • Solution: Klasse – short a (double consonant ss); Lehrer – long e (silent h); spielen – long i (spelled ie); Bäcker – short ä (ck acts as double k)
  • Explanation: On the Sound-Spelling Board, the core rule is: ie / silent h / double vowel = long, double consonant / ck / tz = short.
