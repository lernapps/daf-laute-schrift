## Context: the page “Capitalization of Nouns and Formal Sie”

Capitalize sentence beginnings, all German nouns, and the formal pronoun Sie/Ihr reliably.

### Why Are There So Many Capital Letters?

In Peter's first Year 6 notebook in Lindenberg, Herr Braun marks three spots with a green dot: *'In German, **capitalization** helps the reader's eye right away: every **noun** wears a capital signal!'*

- Exercise `ich-klein-regel` (below)

### The Three Core Capitalization Rules

Sound-Spelling Board: When do I capitalize?:
- Rule | How to spot it | Examples
- 1. Sentence start | First word after a period, !, or ? | **M**orgen fahren wir nach Lindenberg.
- 2. All nouns | Can *der / die / das* or *ein / eine* go before it? | der **T**isch, die **F**reude, das **F**ahrrad
- 3. Formal **Sie** | Addressing adults / strangers (*Sie, Ihnen, Ihr*) | Können **S**ie **I**hr Buch kurz öffnen?

**Remember: Stay lowercase …**

Verbs (*lernen, fahren*), adjectives (*groß, deutsch* in *ein deutsches Buch*), and pronouns **ich**, **du**, **er**, **sie** (*she/they*) stay lowercase mid-sentence.

### Exercise ich-klein-regel

- Task: Peter writes a message to Nadia. Which sentence uses correct German capitalization?
- Answer format: choose one answer
- Choices (✓ right, ✗ wrong, with the app's message):
  - ✓ Heute lerne ich Deutsch in der Schule. – Correct! *Heute* (sentence start), *Deutsch* (language noun), and *Schule* (noun) are capitalized; *ich* is lowercase mid-sentence.
  - ✗ Heute lerne Ich deutsch in der schule. – Watch out: German **ich** is lowercase mid-sentence (unlike English *I*), and the noun *Schule* must be capitalized.
  - ✗ heute lerne ich Deutsch in der Schule. – The first word of a sentence (*Heute*) is always capitalized.
- Solution: Heute lerne ich Deutsch in der Schule.
- Explanation: Notice the key contrast with English: **ich** is lowercase (except at the start of a sentence), whereas **every noun** is capitalized.

### Exercise sie-vs-sie

- Task: In which sentences must **Sie** be capitalized?
- Answer format: choose all right answers
- Choices (✓ right, ✗ wrong, with the app's message):
  - ✓ Entschuldigung, können [Sie] mir den Weg zum Bahnhof zeigen? – Correct! Peter is politely addressing a stranger with formal *Sie*.
  - ✗ Das ist Mina. Morgen spielt [sie] im Sportverein. – Here *sie* means *she* (Mina), so it stays lowercase.
  - ✓ Frau Weber, möchten [Sie] noch einen Kaffee? – Correct! Direct formal address to Frau Weber → always capitalized.
  - ✗ Wo sind die Schüler? Ach, dort kommen [sie] schon! – Here *sie* means *they* (the students), so it stays lowercase.
- Solution: Entschuldigung, können [Sie] mir den Weg zum Bahnhof zeigen?; Frau Weber, möchten [Sie] noch einen Kaffee?
- Explanation: Only formal **Sie / Ihnen / Ihr** is capitalized mid-sentence; *sie* (*she* or *they*) stays lowercase.
