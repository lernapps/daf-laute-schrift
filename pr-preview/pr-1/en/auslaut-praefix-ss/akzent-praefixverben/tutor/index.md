## Context: the page “Stress on Verb Prefixes”

Stress separable verb prefixes (aufstehen, einkaufen) clearly and leave inseparable prefixes (verstehen, beginnen) unstressed.

### AUFstehen vs. verSTEHen: Stress Reveals the Grammar

Sam writes two verbs from Herr Braun's class side by side on the **Sound-Spelling Board**: **AUF**stehen (*get up*) and ver**STEH**en (*understand*). In German, pronunciation and grammar are directly linked here: **if the prefix is stressed, the verb is separable!**

Separable vs. inseparable verb prefixes:
- Prefix type | Where is the stress? | Common prefixes & examples
- **Separable** | always on the **prefix** | *ab-, an-, auf-, aus-, ein-, mit-, vor-, zu-*: **AUF**stehen, **EIN**kaufen, **AN**rufen
- **Inseparable** | on the **verb stem** (prefix unstressed) | *be-, emp-, ent-, er-, ge-, ver-, zer-*: ver**STEH**en, be**GIN**nen, er**ZÄH**len

**Remember: Special case: dual prefixes like um- or über-**

A few prefixes (*durch-, über-, um-, unter-*) even change meaning with stress: **UM**fahren (*knock down*, separable) vs. um**FAH**ren (*drive around*, inseparable); über**SETZ**en (*translate*, inseparable).

### Exercise betonte-praefixe-finden

- Task: In which verbs does the main stress fall on the **prefix** (separable verb)?
- Answer format: choose all right answers
- Choices (✓ right, ✗ wrong, with the app's message):
  - ✓ einkaufen – Correct! **EIN**-kaufen has the stressed separable prefix *ein-* (*Ich kaufe ein*).
  - ✗ beginnen – *be-* is always unstressed and inseparable: be-**GIN**-nen.
  - ✓ mitkommen – Correct! **MIT**-kommen stresses the prefix *mit-* (*Kommst du mit?*).
  - ✗ erklären – *er-* is always unstressed and inseparable: er-**KLÄ**-ren.
- Solution: einkaufen; mitkommen
- Explanation: The eight inseparable prefixes (*be-, emp-, ent-, er-, ge-, miss-, ver-, zer-*) never take main stress; prefixes like *ein-, auf-, an-, mit-* are always stressed.

### Exercise uebersetzen-akzent

- Task: Nadia helps Sam **übersetzen** (*translate*) a sentence into German. Which syllable is stressed?
- Answer format: choose one answer
- Choices (✓ right, ✗ wrong, with the app's message):
  - ✓ über-**SETZ**-en (on the stem — inseparable) – Correct! *übersetzen* meaning *to translate* is inseparable: *Nadia übersetzt den Satz.*
  - ✗ **Ü**-ber-setzen (on the prefix — separable) – With stressed *Ü-ber-*, it means *to ferry across a river*, not *to translate*.
- Solution: über-**SETZ**-en (on the stem — inseparable)
- Explanation: Hearing whether the prefix is stressed tells you right away whether the verb splits in a main clause.
