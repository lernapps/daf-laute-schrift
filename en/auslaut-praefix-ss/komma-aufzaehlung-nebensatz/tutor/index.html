Context: the page “Basic Comma Rules”

Place commas accurately in lists and before subordinate clauses introduced by weil, dass, wenn, or als.

Commas as Clause Signposts in Peter's Email

Peter writes an email to his grandparents about his week in Lindenberg. In German, commas do not simply mark pauses for breath as they often do in English — they mark clear grammar boundaries: lists and subordinate clauses.

  • Exercise komma-weil-und (below)

Sound-Spelling Board: The Two Core A2 Comma Rules

Sound-Spelling Board: The two core A2 comma rules:

  • Rule | Where does the comma go? | Examples
    1. Lists | between list items — no comma before und / oder | Wir kaufen Brötchen**,** Äpfel und Käse.
    1. Subordinate clauses | always before (and after) weil, dass, wenn, als, ob | Ich bleibe zu Hause**, weil** es regnet.
  • Subordinate clause first | comma right between the two verbs | Wenn ich Zeit habe**,** spiele ich Fußball.

Remember: No Oxford comma in German!

In a simple list, German does not put a comma before und or oder: Lukas, Mina und Peter (never Lukas, Mina, und Peter). Before aber and sondern, however, always use a comma!

Exercise komma-weil-und

  • Task: Which sentence from Peter's email has every comma placed correctly?
  • Answer format: choose one answer
  • Choices (✓ right, ✗ wrong, with the app's message):
    • ✓ Ich freue mich, dass ich morgen Nadia, Lukas und Mina treffe. – Correct! A comma before the dass-clause, a comma between Nadia and Lukas, and no comma before und.
    • ✗ Ich freue mich dass ich morgen Nadia, Lukas, und Mina treffe. – This misses the mandatory comma before dass and adds an unwanted Oxford comma before und.
    • ✗ Ich freue mich, dass ich morgen, Nadia Lukas und Mina treffe. – Do not put a comma after morgen; put it between the list items Nadia and Lukas.
  • Solution: Ich freue mich, dass ich morgen Nadia, Lukas und Mina treffe.
  • Explanation: Remember: subordinate clauses (weil, dass, wenn, als) always take a comma; in lists, und / oder replaces the comma.

Exercise komma-pflicht-erkennen

  • Task: In which sentences is a comma required at [ ]?
  • Answer format: choose all right answers
  • Choices (✓ right, ✗ wrong, with the app's message):
    • ✓ Peter nimmt die Deutsche Bahn [ ] weil sein Fahrrad kaputt ist. – Correct! weil introduces a subordinate clause → comma required.
    • ✓ Als Peter in Lindenberg ankam [ ] regnete es stark. – Correct! After an initial subordinate clause, place a comma before the main-clause verb.
    • ✗ Im Rucksack sind ein Heft [ ] und zwei Stifte. – In a simple list, do not put a comma before und.
    • ✓ Ich wollte kommen [ ] aber ich hatte Training. – Correct! Before the contrasting conjunction aber, German always requires a comma.
  • Solution: Peter nimmt die Deutsche Bahn [ ] weil sein Fahrrad kaputt ist.; Als Peter in Lindenberg ankam [ ] regnete es stark.; Ich wollte kommen [ ] aber ich hatte Training.
  • Explanation: Commas separate subordinate clauses (weil, dass, wenn, als) from main clauses and precede aber — but not und in a list.

Exercise komma-regeln-a2-check

  • Task: Order the parts into a sentence starting with a subordinate clause and a comma between the two verbs.
  • Answer format: put the items in the right order (the app shows them shuffled)
  • Solution: 1. Wenn ich Zeit habe,, 2. spiele, 3. ich, 4. Fußball.
  • Explanation: After an initial subordinate clause (Wenn ich Zeit habe,), the comma stands right before the main-clause verb (spiele).

Exercise komma-setzen-a2

  • Task: Choose the right connector (with or without a comma) for Peter's sentences.
  • Answer format: choose a word for each gap
  • How the app counts mistakes of form: capital letters wrong, accents wrong, hyphen wrong, apostrophe wrong, punctuation wrong
  • Items (start → solution):
  1. I am learning German because I live in Lindenberg. → Ich lerne Deutsch, weil ich in Lindenberg wohne.
  2. Lukas, Mina, and Peter ride their bikes. → Lukas, Mina und Peter fahren Fahrrad.
  3. When it rains, we stay inside. → Wenn es regnet, bleiben wir drinnen.
  • Explanation: Always use a comma before weil and after an initial wenn-clause; omit the comma before und in a list.
