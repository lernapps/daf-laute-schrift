Context: the page “Final Consonant Devoicing”

Pronounce final b, d, and g as voiceless p, t, and k and still spell them accurately using the extension test.

Why Does Tag Sound Like Tak?

In German class, Peter asks in surprise: 'Lukas says Tak, Kint, and gelp — yet the book writes Tag, Kind, and gelb!' Herr Braun adds a new tile to the Sound-Spelling Board: at the end of a word or syllable, German voiced consonants always turn voiceless.

  • Exercise auslaut-klang-erkennen (below)

Picture: Peter's Sound-Spelling Board on final devoicing: b turns to p (halb → halbe), d turns to t (Kind → Kinder), g turns to k (Tag → Tage) using the extension test. Caption: Peter's Sound-Spelling Board: extend the word to hear the true final letter.

Final Devoicing and the Extension Test

Final devoicing and the extension test:

  • Spelling → Sound | At word end (voiceless) | Extended (voiced audible)
  • b → [p] | gelb, halb, Urlaub | gelbe, halbe, Urlaube
  • d → [t] | Kind, Freund, Bild | Kinder, Freunde, Bilder
  • g → [k] | Tag, Weg, Zug | Tage, Wege, Züge

Remember: The extension test for spelling

Unsure whether a word ends in b/p, d/t, or g/k? Extend it (plural or -e): Hund → Hunde (so d), bunt → bunte (so t). In -ig endings (fertig, wichtig), Standard German usually pronounces -ig softly like -ich.

Exercise auslaut-klang-erkennen

  • Task: Which pair of German words ends with the exact same sound despite different spelling?
  • Answer format: choose one answer
  • Choices (✓ right, ✗ wrong, with the app's message):
    • ✓ Rad (bike) and Rat (advice) – Correct! Both sound like Raat — only when extended do you hear Räder vs. Räte.
    • ✗ Tage and Taten – Here g and t stand before a vowel (-e / -en), so final devoicing does not apply.
    • ✗ gelbe and helle – In gelbe, b comes before a vowel and stays voiced.
  • Solution: Rad (bike) and Rat (advice)
  • Explanation: Final devoicing happens at the end of a word or syllable (Rad = Rat spoken), not before a vowel (Räder ≠ Räte).

Exercise auslaut-zuordnen

  • Task: Match each word to its final-sound rule.
  • Answer format: match each item on the left with one on the right
  • Solution: Urlaub (Urlaube) – spelled b, pronounced [p]; Hund (Hunde) – spelled d, pronounced [t]; Weg (Wege) – spelled g, pronounced [k]; wichtig (-ig) – ending -ig usually sounds like -ich
